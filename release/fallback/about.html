About Swiftora

Swiftora is an iPhone app for independent secondhand sellers, including thrift-store flippers, vintage sellers, collectors and estate-sale resellers.

Listing work and return on investment

The current app offers listing assistance and ROI tracking. You bring the facts about each item and review what you publish. Swiftora does not connect to marketplaces or automatically publish listings.
