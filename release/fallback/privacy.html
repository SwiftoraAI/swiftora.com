Website privacy

Swiftora LLC operates the website at swiftora.com. This policy covers the website. The Swiftora iPhone app has a separate privacy policy.

Updated September 26, 2026

Browsing this website

You can browse without an account. This website has no signup, upload or contact forms and does not run advertising or analytics scripts. It uses fonts available on your device and locally served graphics.

Previous website visits

An earlier version of this website saved signup details in your browser. Those details may remain in that browser. This version does not read or send them. You can remove them by clearing stored site data in your browser settings.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub's privacy statement explains its handling of this information.

Emailing Swiftora

When you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that correspondence to respond to your request.

Links to other services

Links to the App Store and other external services take you to services with their own privacy practices. The app privacy policy explains information handled by the Swiftora iPhone app.
